Cookie policy

Last updated September 21, 2026

This site does not set tracking, analytics or advertising cookies. It uses your browser's storage for two small settings, described below.

1. What is stored on your device

NameTypePurposeHow long
retra-consentLocal storage, first partyRemembers your choice on the cookie notice, so it does not appear on every pageUntil you clear your browser data or change your choice
retra-themeLocal storage if you chose Accept. Session storage if you chose Essential only.Remembers whether you picked light or dark modeLocal storage: until you clear your browser data. Session storage: until you close the tab.

Neither item holds personal data or is used to identify or track you.

2. Your choices

  • Essential only. The site saves your notice choice. If you switch between light and dark mode, the site keeps that only until you close the tab, then goes back to your device's setting.
  • Accept. The site also remembers your light or dark mode on this device, so it is still there next time.

Use Cookie notice settings in the footer of any page to change your choice. You can also clear this site's data in your browser settings at any time.

3. What is not used

  • No analytics tools.
  • No advertising or social media trackers.
  • No third-party fonts or embeds. Fonts and video are served from this site.

4. Third-party sites

When you follow a link to Fab, TurboSquid, ArtStation or X, you leave this site. Those sites may use their own cookies, described in their own policies.
