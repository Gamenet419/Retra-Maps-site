Refund policy

Last updated September 21, 2026

Retra Maps does not take payments on this website, so refunds are handled by the store where you bought the pack.

1. Where refunds are handled

All purchases are made on third-party stores such as Fab and TurboSquid. Each store sets its own refund rules and processes refunds itself. I cannot issue a refund for a purchase made on a store.

  • For Fab purchases, see the Fab refund policy (opens in a new tab).
  • For TurboSquid and any other store, use the refund or support page of the store where you paid.

2. If a file is wrong or damaged

If a pack has a missing, broken or mislabeled file, send me a message through the contact form with the pack name, the store, your order reference and a short description of the problem. I will look into it and, where I can, send corrected files.

3. Fees

This site adds no fees and no charges of any kind. The price you pay is the price shown by the store at checkout.
