AMP 02: Alpine Glacier Cirque

A glacier cirque terrain in two versions, with 8K heightmaps for each engine, a full set of masks, and textures.

Versions
Glacier and non-glacier
Meshes
14 FBX and OBJ meshes, 7 LODs per version, from about 26 million down to 6,400 vertices
Heightmaps
EXR 32-bit (8192), R16 for Unreal Engine 5 (8129), RAW for Unity (8193)
Masks
12 terrain masks, 16-bit TIFF, 8192 × 8192
Textures
8 textures and 2 normal maps at 8192 × 8192
Works with
Unreal Engine 5, Unity HDRP and URP, Blender, Houdini

AMP 01: Large Scale 8K Alpine Landscape

An 8 km × 8 km alpine basin with snow and bare-rock versions, a full LOD chain, and heightmaps ready for the engine you use.

Size
8 km × 8 km
Versions
Snow and non-snow
Meshes
7 LODs per version, from about 4 million down to 961 vertices
Heightmaps
EXR, R16 and RAW at 8K
Masks
12 terrain masks and a 32-bit normal map
Works with
Unreal Engine 5, Unity HDRP and URP, Blender, Houdini
