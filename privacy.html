Privacy policy

Last updated September 21, 2026

This policy explains what personal data the Retra Maps website (this site) handles, why, and what you can ask me to do about it. It is written for both Nigerian and international visitors.

Short version: this site has no analytics, no advertising, no accounts and no tracking. The only personal data I receive is what you choose to send me through the contact form.

1. Who is responsible

Retra Maps is operated by David Okoi, Lagos, Nigeria. I am the data controller for the data described here. To contact me, use the contact form.

2. What is collected

WhatWhenWhy
Your name, email address, topic and message, plus your two tick-box confirmationsOnly if you send a message with the contact formTo read and reply to you
Your cookie notice choice (retra-consent), stored in your browserWhen you choose Essential only or AcceptTo remember your choice. It contains no personal data.
Your light or dark mode choice (retra-theme), stored in your browserWhen you switch modesTo keep the mode you picked. It is kept on your device if you chose Accept, or only until you close the tab if you chose Essential only.

Technical server logs, such as IP address, browser type and time of requestAutomatically, by the company that hosts this siteRunning and securing the hosting service. I do not use these logs myself.

3. What this site does not do

  • No analytics, advertising or tracking cookies or pixels.
  • No third-party scripts, fonts, social widgets or embedded videos. Fonts and video are hosted on this site.
  • No accounts, no payments and no data sold or shared for marketing.
  • No marketing emails. I only email you in reply to something you sent.

4. How I use your messages, and my legal basis

I use your message only to answer it and to follow up on the same request. Where privacy laws require a legal basis, it is your consent, which you give by ticking the boxes on the form, and my legitimate interest in replying to people who contact me. You can withdraw consent at any time by contacting me.

I keep messages only as long as needed to deal with your request and any follow-up, then delete them, or sooner if you ask.

5. Who else handles data

  • Hosting and contact form service. The company that hosts this site also receives contact form submissions, stores them for me, may apply spam filtering to them, and forwards a notification to my email account. It keeps standard server logs as described above.
  • My email provider. Notifications reach me through my email account, provided by Google, which processes them under its own terms and privacy policy.
  • Stores and social sites. Links to Fab, TurboSquid, ArtStation and X leave this site. Those companies have their own privacy practices, and I do not control them. Purchases and store accounts are handled entirely by the stores.

Because hosting and email providers may store data outside Nigeria, your data can be transferred internationally.

6. Your rights and data deletion requests

You can ask me to tell you what data I hold about you, correct it, delete it, restrict or stop using it, or give you a copy. To ask for deletion, use the data deletion request form. I may need to confirm that the request comes from you. I will reply within 30 days.

If you are unhappy with how I handle your data, you can complain to the Nigeria Data Protection Commission or to the data protection authority in the country where you live.

7. Children

This site and its contact form are not for children. Please do not send personal data if you are under 18. If a parent or guardian believes a child has sent me a message, contact me through the form and I will delete it.

8. Marketing email and unsubscribing

I do not send newsletters or marketing email. If that ever changes, I will ask for your permission first, and every such email will contain a working unsubscribe link.

9. Security

This site is static: it has no database and no login. Messages are protected by the security measures of the hosting and email providers. No system is completely secure, so please do not send sensitive personal information.

10. Changes

If I change this policy, I will update the date at the top of this page.
